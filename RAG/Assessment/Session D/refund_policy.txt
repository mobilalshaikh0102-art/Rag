Food Delivery Refund Policy

Section 1: Eligibility for Refunds
Customers are eligible for a refund if they receive a missing item, a wrong item, or food that arrives in visibly poor condition. Missing item complaints must be reported within 24 hours of delivery through the app's Help section. Wrong item complaints follow the same 24-hour reporting window and require the customer to confirm which item was received instead. Complaints about poor food quality, such as cold, spoiled, or improperly packaged food, require a photo of the item and are reviewed individually by our quality team before a refund is approved.

Section 2: Refund Processing Time
Once a refund request is approved, the amount is credited to the customer's original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds for cancelled orders that were never prepared by the restaurant are typically processed faster, usually within 2 to 3 business days, since no ingredients or labor were consumed.

Section 3: Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds are not issued for change-of-mind cancellations placed after the restaurant has already begun preparing the order. Refunds are also not issued if a customer simply disliked the taste of a correctly prepared item, as this is considered a subjective preference rather than an order error. Orders that were delivered successfully to the address provided by the customer, but which the customer was unavailable to receive, are not eligible for a refund.

Section 4: Partial Refunds
In some cases, only a partial refund is issued. For example, if one item out of a multi-item order was missing, the customer is refunded only for the value of the missing item plus any associated delivery fee proportion, not the entire order total. Partial refunds are also applied when a substituted item was accepted by the customer but was of lower value than the original item ordered.

Section 5: Escalation and Disputes
If a customer disagrees with a refund decision, they may escalate the issue through the "Contact Support" option in the Help section. A live support agent will review the case in more detail, including order history and any submitted photos, and will respond with a final decision within 24 hours of escalation. Repeated refund requests flagged as suspicious may result in a temporary hold on future refund eligibility pending manual review by the trust and safety team.
